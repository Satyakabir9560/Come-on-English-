📖 Complete Grammar - Hindi Me - FULL


1. Noun - संज्ञा
Types: Proper (Ram, Delhi), Common (Boy, City), Collective (Team, Family), Material (Gold, Water), Abstract (Love, Honesty). 
Example: Ram is a good boy. / Honesty is the best policy.


2. Pronoun - सर्वनाम
I, You, He, She, It, We, They, This, That, These, Those, Who, Whom, Whose.
PronounHindiExample
IमैंI am a student.
YouतुमYou are my friend.
Heवह (ladka)He is playing.
Sheवह (ladki)She is dancing.
Theyवे लोगThey are playing.
WeहमWe are students.


3. Verb - क्रिया
V1-V2-V3: Go-Went-Gone, Eat-Ate-Eaten, Write-Wrote-Written, Speak-Spoke-Spoken, Come-Came-Come, Do-Did-Done, Take-Took-Taken, Give-Gave-Given, See-Saw-Seen, Play-Played-Played.


4. Adjective - विशेषण
Good, Big, Beautiful, Tall, Small, Happy, Honest. Example: He is a good boy. / She is beautiful.
Degrees: Good-Better-Best, Big-Bigger-Biggest, Beautiful-More Beautiful-Most Beautiful.


5. Adverb - क्रिया विशेषण
Slowly, Fast, Very, Always, Never. Example: He runs fast. / She speaks slowly.


6. Preposition - सम्बन्ध बोधक
In, On, At, To, From, With, For, Of, By, Under, Over, Between. Example: Book is on the table. / I go to school.


7. Conjunction - संयोजक
And, But, Or, Because, So, Although. Example: Ram and Shyam are friends. / I am late because it is raining.


8. Interjection - विस्मयादि बोधक
Wow! Oh! Hurrah! Alas! Bravo!


9. Articles - A, An, The
A: a boy, a pen. An: an apple, an umbrella (Vowel ke saath). The: the Taj Mahal, the Ganga.


10. Active - Passive Voice
Ram eats a mango. → A mango is eaten by Ram.
She writes a letter. → A letter is written by her.
I am reading a book. → A book is being read by me.


11. Direct - Indirect Speech
He said, "I am happy." → He said that he was happy.
She said, "I will come." → She said that she would come.


12. Question Tags
He is a boy, isn't he? / You are happy, aren't you? / Ram plays, doesn't he?


13. Modal Verbs
Can, Could, May, Might, Must, Should, Would. 
I can speak English. / You should study well. / We must respect our parents.






⏰ All 12 Tenses - COMPLETE - Formula + Hindi + Example
TenseFormulaExample Hindi + English
Present SimpleSub + V1/s/esमैं खाता हूँ - I eat food. / वह खेलता है - He plays.
Present ContinuousSub + is/am/are + V-ingमैं खा रहा हूँ - I am eating. / वह जा रहा है - He is going.
Present PerfectSub + has/have + V3मैंने खा लिया है - I have eaten. / उसने पढ़ लिया है - He has studied.
Present Perfect Cont.Sub + has/have been + V-ingमैं सुबह से खा रहा हूँ - I have been eating since morning.
Past SimpleSub + V2मैंने खाया - I ate food. / वह कल आया - He came yesterday.
Past ContinuousSub + was/were + V-ingमैं खा रहा था - I was eating. / वे खेल रहे थे - They were playing.
Past PerfectSub + had + V3मैंने खा लिया था - I had eaten. / वह जा चुका था - He had gone.
Past Perfect Cont.Sub + had been + V-ingमैं 1 घंटे से खा रहा था - I had been eating for 1 hour.
Future SimpleSub + will + V1मैं खाऊँगा - I will eat. / वह कल आएगा - He will come tomorrow.
Future ContinuousSub + will be + V-ingमैं खा रहा होऊँगा - I will be eating.
Future PerfectSub + will have + V3मैं खा चुका होऊँगा - I will have eaten.
Future Perfect Cont.Sub + will have been + V-ingमैं 1 घंटे से खा रहा होऊँगा - I will have been eating for 1 hour.
💡 Yaad Rakhne ki Trick
Simple = Aadat / Roz ka kaam. Continuous = Chal raha hai. Perfect = Ho chuka hai. Perfect Continuous = Kab se chal raha hai.

✍️ Essay / Letter / Application - COMPLETE


Essay 1: My Best Friend
My best friend's name is Rohan. He is a very good boy. He studies with me in my class. He always helps me in my studies. We play together in the evening. We share our lunch. I love my best friend very much.


Essay 2: Importance of Education
Education is very important in our life. It gives us knowledge and makes us a good person. An educated person can get a good job and respect in society. Education removes darkness. So, we should all study well.


Essay 3: My School
My school is very beautiful. My teachers are very good and kind. My school has a big playground. I have many friends in my school. I love my school very much.


Essay 4: Diwali
Diwali is my favourite festival. It is the festival of lights. We light diyas and eat sweets. We wear new clothes and meet our family. We celebrate Diwali with joy.


Essay 5: My Mother
My mother is the best mother in the world. She loves me very much. She cooks tasty food for me. She helps me in my studies. I respect my mother and I love her very much.


Essay 6: My Village
My village is very beautiful. There are many trees and fields in my village. People of my village are very good and helpful. I love my village.


Essay 7: Importance of Trees
Trees are very important for us. They give us oxygen, fruits and shade. They make our environment clean. We should plant more trees and save them.


Essay 8: My Favourite Teacher
My favourite teacher is Ramesh Sir. He teaches us English. He is very kind and honest. He always helps weak students. I respect him very much.


Essay 9: Health is Wealth
Health is wealth. If we are healthy, we can do everything. We should eat good food, do exercise and sleep on time. We should not eat junk food.


Essay 10: My Aim in Life
My aim in life is to become a teacher. I want to teach poor children for free. I want to make my parents proud. I will work hard to achieve my aim.
